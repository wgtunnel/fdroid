What's new:
- Advanced tunnel DNS options like split and encrypted tunnel DNS
- IP4P support for servers behind CGNAT
- Bugfix for auto tunnel that sometimes caused rapid toggle of tunnels
- Bugfix for the tunnel stats job high CPU and battery usage on some devices
- Various other bugfixes and improvements

SHA-256 fingerprints for the 4096-bit signing certificate:
```sh
5204d82e766e8aa14dcbb06dc70aebae2bdd812d4d6203cd521a8a685d7d3d80
```

To verify fingerprint:
```sh
apksigner verify --print-certs [path to APK file] | grep SHA-256
```
