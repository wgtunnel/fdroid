What's new:
- Seamless tunnel recovery feature for tunnel connectivity recovery without dropping VPN protection
- Improved tile integration with tunnel statuses
- Toggle tunnel action added for remote control feature
- Auto-tunnel Wi-Fi detection bugfix on VPN starts
- Translations
