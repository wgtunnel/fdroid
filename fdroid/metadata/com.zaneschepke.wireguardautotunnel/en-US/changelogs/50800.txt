What's new:
- Multihop tunnel in a tunnel support
- Tunnel groups
- Bugfixes for IPv6 local proxy
- Bugfixes for auto tunnel (no location permissions, Android Auto, OEM specific quirks)
- Split tunnel and DNS settings UX improvements
- Various other improvements and fixes

SHA-256 fingerprints for the 4096-bit signing certificate:
```sh
5204d82e766e8aa14dcbb06dc70aebae2bdd812d4d6203cd521a8a685d7d3d80
```

To verify fingerprint:
```sh
apksigner verify --print-certs [path to APK file] | grep SHA-256
```
