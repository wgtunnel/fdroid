What's new:
- Seamless recovery configurable delay and stats
- Tunnel recovery features can now be applied while a tunnel is active
- Bugfixes for peer endpoints resolution of IPv6 only endpoints
- Bugfixes for failed parsing of valid Amnezia packet signatures
- Various other fixes and improvements

To verify fingerprint:
```sh
apksigner verify --print-certs [path to APK file] | grep SHA-256
```
