What's new:
- Auto tunnel by BSSID feature with wildcard support
- Auto tunnel specificity support for conflicting rule matches
- Seamless roaming feature for tunnel recovery on Wi-Fi access point and airplane mode migrations
- Bugfixes for DNS resolution and tunnel hanshake issues
- Various other fixes and improvements

SHA-256 fingerprints for the 4096-bit signing certificate:
```sh
5204d82e766e8aa14dcbb06dc70aebae2bdd812d4d6203cd521a8a685d7d3d80
```

To verify fingerprint:
```sh
apksigner verify --print-certs [path to APK file] | grep SHA-256
```
